How we work

Custom development helps when your site needs to do more than publish information. Tell us how your team handles inquiries, bookings, payments, or updates today so we can scope the work around the people using it.

  1. 1. Define the workflow

    We map what visitors do, what your staff needs to see, and where the information goes. For integrations, we check which systems have suitable APIs and what access the project needs.

  2. 2. Agree on scope and ownership

    We document the features, how you’ll review them, and which accounts or services you’ll own. Existing code and migrations get a review so we can account for data, URLs, and dependencies.

  3. 3. Build and review

    We work through the main flows first, then check permission boundaries, error states, and common mobile paths. You can review the experience before it goes live.

  4. 4. Deploy and hand off

    We agree on the launch plan and check the live flows after deployment. We document how to access and maintain the build and discuss what support your team needs.
