How we work

A good fit for shops, restaurants, and service businesses that need a clear, trustworthy website customers can use on a phone. Bring a new idea or an existing site that has outgrown its layout.

  1. 1. Map the customer journey

    We identify the one or two actions that matter most, such as booking, requesting a quote, or viewing a menu. Then we plan pages around those actions and list the photos, copy, and business details we’ll need.

  2. 2. Design the important pages

    We start with the homepage and the page customers rely on most. You can review how both look and read on a phone before we carry the design through to the rest of the site.

  3. 3. Build and check the details

    We build the approved pages, then check links, forms, keyboard navigation, and image sizing. For a redesign, we identify useful existing URLs and plan what happens to each one.

  4. 4. Launch and hand off

    We review the live pages and show you how to handle routine updates. If your team needs help after launch, we can scope hosting and maintenance with you.
